# Post-mortem: <one-line title>

**Blameless.** We describe what the *system and process* allowed, not who
slipped. Write "the release had no test for VIP wristbands", never "Riya forgot".

## Summary

Two or three sentences: what broke, for whom, for how long, and how it was fixed.

## Impact

Who was affected, how many requests failed, and for how long (minutes).
